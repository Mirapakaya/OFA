OFA — One For All is a powerful, local-first Android toolkit that keeps your files and data on your device.

Key features
- PDF: merge, split, rotate, delete pages, extract text, protect with password, reorder pages, compress, convert to images.
- Images: compress, resize, crop, rotate, flip, convert, remove EXIF, view EXIF.
- Documents & text: case converter, word counter, diff, sort, deduplicate, markdown/HTML, JSON/YAML/XML tools, regex tester, and more.
- Developer: base64, url/html encoding, hash, UUID, JWT decode, cron tools, timestamp, DNS, subnet/CIDR, secret scanners.
- Data & CSV: CSV to JSON/SQL, cleaner, sorter, filter, merge, split, deduplicate.
- Business & education: invoices, quotations, purchase orders, certificates, ID cards, mark sheets, timetables, worksheets.
- Calculators: EMI, GST, interest, loan, BMI, unit converter, engineering calculators, age, date, percentage, tip.
- QR & barcode generation.
- Privacy: password generator, password strength auditor, PII detector.
- Local notes, emergency SOS, compass, weather, offline maps framework, and local E2EE messaging.
- Local backup & restore to keep your data portable.

OFA processes data locally whenever possible. Network-dependent features are clearly marked and only send data to the external provider you choose, never to OFA servers.
