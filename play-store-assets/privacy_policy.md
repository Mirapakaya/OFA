# OFA Privacy Policy

OFA ("One For All") is designed with privacy in mind.

- **Local-first**: most tools process data directly on your device.
- **No cloud**: OFA does not operate cloud storage or servers.
- **External providers**: network-dependent features may send data to the third-party provider you select. OFA does not control those providers.
- **Data deletion**: use Settings → Delete all OFA data to remove local data.

For questions, contact the developer through the app's About screen.
